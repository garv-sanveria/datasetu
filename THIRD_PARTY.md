## 2. Public Data and Administrative Gazetteers

- **Local Government Directory of India (LGD)**:
  - Source: [lgdirectory.gov.in](https://lgdirectory.gov.in) (Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Government of India).
  - Seed dataset (`datasetu/data/lgd_districts.json`) contains an authoritative sample of canonical State and District codes used for cross-walking public spreadsheets.
- **Synthetic Demo Data**:
  - `demo/scheme_spend_messy.csv` and `demo/scheme_spend_messy.xlsx` are **100% synthetic**, generated by `scripts/make_demo_data.py` to replicate real structural formatting patterns found in data.gov.in open datasets.
